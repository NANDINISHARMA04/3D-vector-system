Vector

Hold up your hand in front of the webcam. Snap to summon a model made of particles, open your hand to explode it, point at parts to learn what they do.

Video is processed on your device with MediaPipe and never leaves your browser. Without a camera: drag to rotate, scroll to zoom, click parts, Space to snap.

Controls

Hand

  • 🫰 Snap — summon / dissolve
  • ✊ Fist — assemble
  • 🖐️ Open hand — exploded view
  • 🔄 Twist wrist — spin · raise/lower — tilt
  • ☝️ Point — identify a part
  • 🤏 Pinch on a part — drag it out
  • 🙌 Two hands — zoom
  • ✌️ Peace — next model

Mouse & keys

  • Drag — rotate · Scroll — zoom
  • Hover — identify · Drag a part — pull out
  • Space snap · F assemble · E explode
  • N/→ next · ← previous
  • R reset · A auto-rotate · C section
  • T tour · S screenshot · V camera
